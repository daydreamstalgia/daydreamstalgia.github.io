DM Inventory – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

DM Inventory (“the app”, package ro.daydreamstalgia.duelmastersinventory) is a free, unofficial, fan-made collection tracker developed by an independent developer (“daydreamstalgia”, “we”). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

In short: the app does not collect, sell or share personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data you enter

Your collection, transactions, notes, prices and settings are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. We never receive this data. You can delete it at any time from the app’s settings, by clearing the app’s data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

The app connects to the internet only to download card set data (card lists and images) that you choose to import, from daydreamstalgia.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. These requests send no personal information from the app. As with any website, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other websites

If you tap a parcel tracking number you saved on a transaction, it opens in your web browser on a third-party tracking site (parcelsapp.com). Only the tracking number is sent, and only when you tap it. That site’s own privacy policy applies.

Notifications

The app may show a notification while it downloads or imports card data in the background, so you can see the progress. Notifications are generated on your device and you can turn them off in Android settings.

Device backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This is controlled by you and by Google, under Google’s privacy policy; we have no access to these backups.

Data sharing and selling

We do not collect personal data, so we do not share or sell it to anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
